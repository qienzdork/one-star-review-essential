# One-Star Review of Something Essential

A deadpan one-star review of sleep, the everyday essential that takes a
third of your life and still sends you back to work tired. `main.py` is a
small deterministic program (stdlib only, no network, no randomness) that
writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`, or to `$OUTPUT_PATH` when set.

The review: *"One star. Sleep takes a third of my life, gives me dreams
about being late for exams I finished years ago, and still sends me back
to work tired. No refund."*
